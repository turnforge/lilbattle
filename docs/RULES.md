There are 6 things you can do during your turn:

Build - Click or tap on a base that is under your control (your team color) and build a unit. You can only build units you can afford. You'll earn coins each round and for each base you control.
Move - Units can move 1-6 spaces based on the terrain and type of unit. Units can move then attack, move or attack and others can move, attack, and move again ("retreat").
Attack - Each unit can cause different amounts of damage depending on the unit type and the terrain it is on. If multiple of your units attack the same enemy then a wound bonus is inflicted. (See: Attack Calculations)
Fix - Some units can fix other units by restoring their health points. If you are new to the game you likely won't have access to these units which are available in the shop. (See: Fix Calculations)
Capture - Soldiers and hovercrafts can capture bases. Move one of these units to a base and click/tap once to begin capturing the base. If your unit survives until your next turn the base is yours! You can start building units and earning coins for your new base.
Heal - If you decide not to move or attack with a unit you can heal. Unused units are automatically healed at the end of your turn. If you wish to manually heal you can tap on the unit and tap the blue heal button next to the unit stats. Healing amounts vary by unit and the terrain type. Air units can only heal on Air Bases. Units cannot be healed on enemy bases.
Once you are done commanding your army click End Turn. Your opponent will be notified it is their turn.
